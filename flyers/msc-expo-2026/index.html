How CAM+ works

Wireless sensors
Transmitter outside the unit, calibrated probe inside. Fitted in minutes.
WARP on site
Stores data locally, sounds an audible alarm and forwards to the cloud.
CAM+ cloud portal
Every site in one live view: tamper-proof records, reports, certificates.
Alarm → audit
Call, SMS and email escalate until accepted; each alarm closes with a signed audit.

What you can monitor

Fridges & freezers
PT1000 probes, −100 to +200°C; long-cable option for cold rooms
LN₂ & cryogenic
Tank and flask probes down to −200°C for cell and tissue banking
Incubators
Combined temperature and humidity on a single umbilical probe
Rooms & stores
Ambient temperature and 0–100% RH for labs and drug stores
Doors
Temperature plus door-contact on one sensor — catch the door left ajar
O₂, CO₂ & pressure
Differential pressure and gas levels via 4–20 mA transducer inputs
Power & equipment faults
Mains failure, compressor trip, fridge and incubator alarm contacts
Mapping & validation
Mapping sensors for fridge, freezer and warehouse qualification
